Ferrolite needed a system flexible enough to sell fourteen typefaces without each one fighting the last for attention. The answer was a single rigid grid and a rotating cast of rust, iron, and slag-inspired colorways assigned per release.

I also drew the foundry's own display face, Ferrolite Condensed, used exclusively across the storefront.
