Kilnhouse asked for a mark that could survive being pressed into wet clay. The final logotype is cut from a single stamp, so every mug, bowl, and shipping label carries the same slightly-imperfect impression — no two are pixel-identical, which is the point.

The site runs on a lean stack with restock alerts baked into the product pages, since drops used to crash the studio's old Squarespace checkout within ninety seconds.
