The whole identity had to work at two scales: a business card and a hand-painted door sign visible from across the street. I built the wordmark first as a two-inch stencil, then scaled everything else around what still read clearly at that size.

The digital menu is a single page the chef edits from a phone between services — no CMS login screen, just a text field behind a passcode only the kitchen knows.
