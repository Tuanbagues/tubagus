Three hundred and eight photographs, shot at the same coordinates every low tide for six years, edited down to ninety-one. The book is smyth-sewn so it lies flat, which mattered more to me than almost any other decision on the project.

The microsite translates the sequencing into a scroll-driven piece — no autoplay, no music, just the tide moving at the speed you scroll.
